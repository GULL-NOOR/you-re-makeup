Frequently Asked Questions

Find rapid answers to inquiries regarding our formulations, delivery, and boutique return policy.

Are all You're Makeup products 100% cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes, absolutely. We hold international certification through Leaping Bunny and PETA. We never test on animals at any stage of development, nor do we sell in markets requiring animal testing. All ingredients are vegan and free from parabens, mineral oils, phthalates, and synthetic fragrance.

What is your shipping timeline and return policy?

We provide complimentary VIP shipping worldwide for all orders exceeding $50. Standard domestic delivery arrives within 2-3 business days. We offer a 30-Day Luxury Happiness Guarantee: if a shade doesn't suit your complexion, return it seamlessly for a full refund or exchange.

How do I get an exact virtual shade match?

Our Parisian-trained beauty concierges are available 6 days a week. You can submit photos in natural daylight through our contact form above, selecting "Complimentary Shade Matching", and our artists will recommend your flawless foundation and lip pairings within 3 hours.

Are You're Makeup cases and compacts refillable?

Yes! Our 2026 collection features magnetic weighted housings engineered for sustainability. Once you empty your Velvet Siren lipstick or Silk Veil powder, simply click in a fresh refill cartridge at a preferential VIP price.
