Our Genesis

Redefining Luxury Makeup For The Modern Era

Founded in Paris with a defiant studio in Beverly Hills, YOU'RE MAKEUP began with a single radical principle: luxury beauty should never force you to compromise between fierce pigment payoff and clean, skin-nurturing ingredients.

Tired of chalky formulas that creased under bright strobe lights, our visionary cosmetic chemists spent five years engineering our proprietary Velvet-Luxe™ matrix. The result? Featherlight formulas with unyielding 24-hour endurance and hyper-saturated chromatic tones.

Today, You're Makeup stands as the ultimate sanctuary for beauty collectors, celebrity artists, and bold individuals worldwide who understand that glamour is not an afterthought—it is your signature armor.
